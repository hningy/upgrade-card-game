# 升级牌面中央插画：AI 生成提示词

## 交付目标

只需要生成牌面中央插画，不要生成完整的扑克牌。

程序会继续绘制外层卡片、圆角、四角点数、花色、主牌标记和选中状态，因此图片中不要出现这些内容。

建议先提供 6 张：

1. `ace-ornament.png`
2. `jack-court.png`
3. `queen-court.png`
4. `king-court.png`
5. `joker-big.png`
6. `joker-small.png`

如果图片 AI 很擅长保持一致性，也可以每张分别生成四种花色版本，共 18 张；命名规则见文末。

## 统一技术要求

- PNG，透明背景。
- 竖版 3:4，建议 `900 × 1200` 或更高。
- 单个完整图案，不要拼图、不要多张牌。
- 图案居中，四周保留约 8% 透明安全边距。
- 不要卡片边框、四角字母、花色点数、阴影背景。
- 不要文字、数字、签名、水印或 logo。
- 在约 60 像素高时仍能看清主要轮廓。
- 不要照片、3D 卡片模型、桌面或手持效果。

## 统一风格前缀

将下面这段放在每个提示词开头：

```text
Create one isolated decorative illustration on a fully transparent canvas. The canvas is only a transparent workspace and must not resemble paper, a card, a plaque, a sign, or a framed object.
Style: refined classical European royal court illustration, combining crisp vector shapes with delicate antique engraving lines. Symmetrical, elegant, highly legible at small size, polished but not photorealistic.
Palette: deep charcoal ink, warm antique gold, subtle ivory highlights, and restrained crimson accents.
Composition: one centered portrait or emblem, front-facing, contained entirely inside the transparent canvas with generous safe margins.
Technical: PNG, square 1:1 canvas, 1024x1024 or higher, fully transparent background outside the artwork itself. Only the drawn ornament and character should be visible. No ivory or white rectangular background panel.
Avoid: outer frame, rectangular container, paper or card shape, corner indices, extra letters, numbers, suit pips, watermark, logo, signature, collage, 3D mockup, photo, cropped head or cropped ornament. The only exception is the large decorative letter A explicitly requested below.
```

## 各牌提示词

### A

追加：

```text
Subject: one large decorative uppercase letter "A" as the centerpiece. The letter itself must be clearly legible and prominently scaled. Decorate the A with elegant symmetrical filigree, scrolling vines, small laurel leaves, and restrained antique-gold linework. This is an isolated ornamental lettering illustration, not a card and not an empty medallion. No suit symbol. Only one letter A appears. Absolutely no enclosing border, rectangle, ivory panel, or second small A.
```

### J

追加：

```text
Subject: a stylized male Jack court figure, youthful and alert, wearing a decorated cap with a short feather. Symmetrical bust portrait with geometric robes and subtle antique-gold trim. No text, no sword crossing outside the canvas.
```

### Q

追加：

```text
Subject: a stylized Queen court figure wearing an elegant tiara and structured royal collar. Calm confident expression, symmetrical bust composition, geometric fabric patterns, delicate antique-gold details, classic playing-card elegance.
```

### K

追加：

```text
Subject: a stylized King court figure with a tall ornate crown and short sculpted beard. Symmetrical regal bust, layered ceremonial robe, strong readable silhouette, restrained crimson and antique-gold accents, classic European playing-card style.
```

### 大王

追加：

```text
Subject: a majestic jester bust with a tall two-pronged cap, small bells, expressive confident smile, and an ornate gold collar. Use warm crimson and antique gold as the dominant accents. Playful, premium and clearly different from the small joker.
```

### 小王

追加：

```text
Subject: a clever silver-and-ink jester bust with a slightly lower cap, tiny bells, mischievous grin, and a geometric collar. Use cool silver-blue, ivory and charcoal accents. Playful and premium, visually distinct from the big joker but from the same card series.
```
